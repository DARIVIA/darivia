PROBLEM

The outcome doesn't explain the decision
Brands see outcomes - sales, conversion, returns, markdowns. But they rarely see the decision process that produced them: why a product entered consideration, why it was rejected, what displaced it, and how context changed the choice.

GAP

Product fit is not competitive consideration
A product can fit a consumer's needs and still never enter serious consideration.

Choice emerges from the interaction between Consumer - identity, preferences, parameters; Mission - goal, context, constraints; and Product - attributes, positioning, alternatives.

Traditional customer and product data capture parts of this system. They rarely capture the trajectory of the decision itself.

MARKET SHIFT

LLMs are becoming a new interface for commerce, but they do not by themselves create durable brand knowledge about choice.

A model can conduct a good conversation while the brand still loses the context, reasons, and decision patterns after the session ends.
A new intelligence layer between customer data and commercial outcomes
