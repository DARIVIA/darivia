DARIVIA - Personal Product Advisor

Understand what genuinely fits your goals, context and priorities before turning a complex choice into a shortlist.

From a request to a confident decision

The advisor helps with personally consequential choices across products, services, destinations, providers, plans and packages. It clarifies what matters, researches relevant options when appropriate and makes trade-offs visible.

Your current choice comes first

The active request, goal and circumstances determine the consultation. A saved profile provides prior context but does not replace what you say in the current conversation.

Continuity with your permission

When you connect a saved profile, the advisor can use the permitted personal context to reduce repetition and support continuity between choices.

Research when it is useful

The advisor can research current options and prefer official product or provider pages when they are available.

You remain in control

You can review or correct relevant context, disconnect access and request deletion. The advisor does not complete purchases or handle payment details.

Access and current release

The public release is being prepared. Access requires an account created with an email address and password, plus explicit consent to the collection and processing of data described in the Privacy Policy. After the first successful sign-in, users may add a passkey on supported devices; the password remains available as a fallback. Some connected functions remain demonstrations until a specific pilot integration is enabled.

Recommendations are decision support, not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or other regulated advice.
