DARIVIA - Personal Advisor

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Understand what genuinely fits your goals, context and priorities before turning a complex choice into a carefully selected Set of the most suitable options and meaningful alternatives.

From a request to a confident decision

DARIVIA - Personal Advisor helps ChatGPT clarify complex, nonmedical consumer choices involving products, services or destinations, using an expert methodology and your authorized DARIVIA Consumer Profile. Examples include clothing, shoes, watches, accessories, cars and travel. It clarifies what matters, investigates relevant options and makes trade-offs visible.

Your current choice comes first

The active request, goal and circumstances determine the consultation. Your DARIVIA Consumer Profile provides prior context but does not replace what you say in the current conversation.

Continuity through authorization

Before you grant access, the authorization notice explains profile use, structured decision history, relevant data categories and processing by service providers. Authorizing permits DARIVIA to create, save, use and update these records for this and future authorized choices. Relevant nonmedical sizes, measurements, body proportions and fit preferences can be included where necessary. See the Privacy Policy. Without authorization, the connected advisor cannot start.

Research when it is useful

The advisor can research current options and prefer official product or provider pages when they are available.

You remain in control

You can review or correct relevant context, disconnect access and request deletion. The advisor does not complete purchases or handle payment details.

Access and current release

The public release is being prepared. Access uses a DARIVIA account authenticated through WorkOS AuthKit with email and password. A first or last name is not required, and social login and Enterprise SSO are not offered. The saved profile provides continuity, while each current request defines its own goal and circumstances. Structured trajectories and version history are retained; a new chat is not a request to erase prior choices. DARIVIA does not retrieve the full ChatGPT conversation through its tools.

Scope: medical-purpose choices, medical goods, treatments, clinical providers and health-insurance workflows, comparisons or recommendations are outside DARIVIA's scope. Other prohibited or unsupported purposes are also excluded; see the Terms of Service. Incidental health context does not automatically prohibit an ordinary consumer choice, but medical records and personal health facts must not be sent to DARIVIA. For example, ordinary clothing fit and a separately agreed frame-only glasses choice can use necessary nonmedical dimensions; treatment selection and prescription-lens advice cannot.

ChatGPT conducts the conversation and produces the response using DARIVIA's methodology and permitted context. The methodology does not override OpenAI's rules. The advisor does not sell products, place orders or process payments. Its scope limits actual functionality; a general disclaimer is not permission to run an excluded workflow.
