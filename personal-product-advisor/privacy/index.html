Privacy Policy

How DARIVIA - Personal Advisor processes information for authorized consumer choices.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who provides the service

DARIVIA - Personal Advisor is published by Yuri Khechoyan and powered by DTI. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to contact@darivia.com.

The advisor supports nonmedical consumer choices. Medical-purpose selection, medical goods, treatments, clinical providers and health-insurance workflows, comparisons and recommendations are outside its scope. See the Terms of Service for the full scope restrictions.

2. Information processed and its purposes

DARIVIA receives task-specific information intentionally sent by ChatGPT through the connected tools, rather than access to your entire conversation or unrelated accounts. This can include:

  • Choice information: your request, desired result, budget, relevant circumstances, expressed preferences, values, references and practical use requirements. These support understanding and evaluating the current choice.
  • Nonmedical physical and appearance information: where necessary, height, clothing or shoe size, body measurements and proportions, fit and coverage preferences, and relevant visible colouring described for a style choice. These can be personally sensitive and are used for practical fit and appearance, not to infer a diagnosis, pregnancy, ethnicity or other health or protected status. You may decline to provide optional details; the advisor should explain any resulting limits rather than invent them.
  • Decision history: structured choice events, evidence, goals, circumstances, evaluations, outcomes and version history. These records form your Consumer Decision Trajectories. Situational goals and circumstances remain part of the relevant trajectory, not permanent personality traits.
  • DARIVIA Consumer Profile: permitted stable characteristics, preferences, values and reference meanings, together with relevant interpretation and version lineage, used for this and future authorized choices. The current request can correct or qualify earlier information.
  • Account and access information: verified account identifiers, application grants and token-validation information used to authenticate access, associate your authorized profile and enforce access revocation. Auth0 separately processes the account information used for sign-in.
  • Operational information: request and job identifiers, timestamps, status and error codes, usage and validation records used for access control, reliability, investigation and abuse prevention. Hosting and identity providers may also process IP addresses and connection metadata under their policies.
  • Optional feedback: three Yes/No answers and an optional comment, submitted through the separate feedback form described below.

The DARIVIA tools must not request, collect or process payment-card data, protected health information (PHI), government identification numbers, passwords, authentication codes, private keys or similar access secrets. Medical records and personal health facts are also outside this advisor's data scope. Names, contact details, delivery addresses, raw conversation transcripts and unnecessary personal information do not belong in the choice payload or Consumer Profile. Support correspondence and the identity-provider sign-in flow are separate from advisory tools.

Relevant ordinary body measurements and fit preferences are not automatically medical information. However, wording something as a consumer preference does not make a health fact permissible. If information falls within a regulated sensitive or special category under applicable law, it must not be sent unless its necessity, legally adequate consent and prominent disclosure have first been established. This version does not enable an additional regulated-sensitive category. Consent does not override the exclusions above.

DARIVIA does not retrieve, store or reconstruct the full ChatGPT chat log through its tools. Structured trajectories are nevertheless retained; they are not raw conversation transcripts. A photo uploaded to ChatGPT is handled by the host under its own policies. This advisory workflow does not require the original photo to be copied into DARIVIA; only necessary permitted observations may be shared.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide the requested decision support, maintain your authorized profile and complete decision history, preserve continuity across choices, validate updates, protect and diagnose the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Profile personalization is limited to the authorized consumer-choice function; it is not used to infer unrelated protected characteristics. The service does not sell personal information.

The connected advisor does not use profile, trajectory or feedback data to train models. Processing by the ChatGPT host is governed separately by your account or workspace settings and OpenAI's terms.

4. Your authorization

Use of the connected advisor requires a DARIVIA account authenticated through Auth0 by Okta. Account authentication and permission to use your profile are distinct purposes, even when presented within the same authorization flow.

Before you grant access, the authorization notice explains that DARIVIA creates, saves, uses and updates your Consumer Profile and structured decision history for this and future authorized choices, names the relevant data categories and explains processing by service providers. Choosing to authorize grants this connected advisor that access. If you decline, no new grant is made and the connected advisor cannot start. Separate permission is not requested after every normal profile update covered by your authorization; host confirmations may still be required.

Signing in, holding a token or refreshing it in the background does not by itself establish new consent to a material expansion of data collection. Publishing an updated policy alone does not establish that you saw or accepted an expanded permission.

The authorization grant records the permission given to this connected advisor and can be revoked. The advisor does not receive access to unrelated accounts, brand systems or payment information.

5. Service providers and hosts

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the product:

  • OpenAI: hosts the ChatGPT conversation. Separately, DARIVIA uses the OpenAI API to interpret permitted choice evidence, validate updates, generate profile wording and determine which permitted profile information is relevant to a request. Task-specific evidence or approved profile meanings may therefore be processed by external models, not only by the model in your chat.
  • Auth0 by Okta: authenticates your account. Account credentials belong only in its sign-in flow, not in a DARIVIA advisory tool call.
  • Render: hosts the DARIVIA/DTI service and its persistent data stores in the Oregon, USA region. This service location is not a guarantee that every provider or support system processes data only there.
  • GitHub Pages: serves public product and legal pages. Visiting a page exposes normal connection metadata to the hosting provider; it does not grant access to your DARIVIA Consumer Profile.

Only data required for a processing role should be sent to it. Profile-relevance processing may examine all distinct permitted candidate meanings to decide which are relevant; the resulting chat response should contain only the relevant information. Removing duplicate wording and technical fields does not mean that the provider sees no personal data.

DARIVIA configures its model calls with response storage disabled. This is not a promise of Zero Data Retention: provider abuse-monitoring, security and prompt-caching retention can still apply under the provider's policy and account configuration. See OpenAI API data controls. The host's chat-history settings remain separate.

These product and legal pages do not load analytics. Any analytics on the main DARIVIA website is governed by its separate analytics controls and notice.

6. Retention and deletion

Your Consumer Profile, structured trajectories and their version and validation history are retained for authorized continuity, including when a choice is completed or a new chat begins. There is no automatic expiry or scheduled deletion of these records. Revoking one application's access stops that grant; it does not erase the profile or its history. Optional feedback and annual invitation records likewise have no fixed expiry; a later annual review does not replace an earlier one.

Different technical copies have different lifetimes:

  • Render-hosted runtime logs: the current Hobby workspace provides a seven-day log-retention window. This does not set the lifetime of records stored in DARIVIA's database or separately saved diagnostic files.
  • Render disk snapshots: Render documents daily snapshots available for seven days after capture. These are distinct from DARIVIA's own recovery copies stored on its disk. See Render disk snapshots and Render log retention.
  • DARIVIA technical database records: processing and validation receipts, version lineage and access-control records have no scheduled expiry. They are retained to preserve decision history, verify updates, prevent duplicate processing, investigate errors and enforce access boundaries. A current working state can be replaced as a choice progresses; that is not deletion of the canonical trajectory history. The seven-day Render window does not apply to these database records.
  • DARIVIA recovery copies: application-created database backups and retained recovery exports have no fixed expiry or automatic rotation. They are kept for recovery, verification and investigation, with administrative access restricted. They are not a separate source of routine personalization. These copies are different from Render's seven-day disk snapshots; a newer backup does not automatically remove an older one.
  • Temporary processing copies: some job statuses, startup responses and processing packages remain in the running process until it ends or restarts; they are not guaranteed to disappear immediately after an answer. This process lifetime is not a deletion deadline for related database records or backups.
  • Permission and form validity: token, authorization-code or invitation expiry limits whether the credential or link can still be used; it does not itself delete the associated record. A feedback invitation is valid for 24 hours, while the annual invitation record and submitted feedback have no automatic expiry.
  • Auth0 tenant logs: the current Free plan provides a one-day tenant-log retention window. Account records and authorization grants are separate from these logs. See Auth0's plan retention details.
  • Provider-side model processing: OpenAI's default API abuse-monitoring retention is up to 30 days, with its stated legal and safety exceptions. Provider prompt caching can retain application state for up to 24 hours under the documented model behavior. These are provider rules, not DARIVIA database-expiry settings. No approved Zero Data Retention configuration is promised; see OpenAI API data controls.

You may request deletion of your Consumer Profile and associated DARIVIA records by contacting contact@darivia.com. Requests require appropriate identity verification and are handled subject to applicable law. This is not a promise of immediate deletion from every backup or from independently operated host/provider systems. We explain any applicable lawful retention or technical limits when handling a request.

There is no automated all-copy deletion process. A verified privacy request must be assessed across the active records and relevant recovery copies, with any necessary legal retention and technical limitations explained to you. Any later restoration must account for previously resolved privacy requests so that it does not undo an agreed correction or deletion. A valid request is not treated as automatically satisfied by disconnecting the app, expiring a token or removing only the displayed profile. This notice does not introduce automatic cleanup or change the stored trajectories.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to view your current DARIVIA Consumer Profile, correct permitted information through the ordinary advisor dialogue, revoke this application's access, and make a privacy request at contact@darivia.com. Profile viewing, access revocation and privacy-request guidance remain available even when a choice is outside the advisor's scope. Revoking one application's access does not revoke another separately authorized application's access. Depending on your location, additional access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, consent-withdrawal and complaint rights may apply.

Declining optional physical details or feedback does not authorize guesses or make feedback a condition of advice. If you do not want ongoing profile processing, revoke the application's access and contact us about the information already held. Withdrawal does not itself erase earlier records or make prior lawful processing unlawful.

8. Security and international processing

Safeguards include authenticated access, application-specific grants, task-specific data preparation and validation at processing and storage boundaries. Excluded information must be removed by the host before transmission; server checks are a further safeguard, not a guarantee that every possible sensitive statement will be detected. Do not submit prohibited information for the server to clean. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Our Render service is located in Oregon, USA. Other providers may process information in other countries, and a globally configured API project is not a promise of EU or single-country residency.

Provider information on international processing and safeguards is available in the OpenAI Data Processing Addendum, Okta agreements and trust documents, Render Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. These describe the providers' respective practices; they are not a promise of a particular data-residency option or certification for DARIVIA. Contact contact@darivia.com for information about processing locations or safeguards relevant to your account.

9. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, or to a higher minimum age where local law requires it.

10. Optional feedback

After a Buyer Decision Image has been formed, the advisor may offer a voluntary, single-screen form containing three Yes/No questions and an optional comment. It is offered no more than once in 365 days per authorized DARIVIA user across chats and applications. Feedback is stored separately from the Consumer Profile and is not choice evidence. An internal pseudonymous identifier supports the annual limit; collection is not represented as fully anonymous.

Submitting the form permits DARIVIA to assess quality and usefulness, research the user experience, find errors, improve the product and methodology, and produce statistics and reports. Feedback may be quoted or published in DARIVIA's website, social media, advertising and presentations to customers, partners and investors, and may be shortened or translated without changing its meaning. Account, profile and conversation data are not included in a publication or supplied with the feedback. Do not include personal or confidential information in the comment. ChatGPT checks a comment before permitted text is sent to DARIVIA. Declining feedback does not affect the consultation.

The feedback notice appears with the form before submission. Feedback is not used to train models. An authorization grant does not itself submit feedback.

11. Changes

We update the policy when relevant practices change and identify the published version by date. Material changes that need a new disclosure or permission are not authorized merely by updating this page or refreshing an existing token.
