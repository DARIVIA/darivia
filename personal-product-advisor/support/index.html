Help and contact

Support

Help with access, profile connection, privacy requests and unexpected advisor behaviour.

Contact

Email contact@darivia.com. Include a concise description of what happened, the approximate time and your ChatGPT platform and device. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information or full conversation exports.

Connection problems

Confirm that the DARIVIA advisor is selected in ChatGPT. If prompted, reconnect the app and sign in with your email address and password or an enrolled passkey. If the connection fails again, send the approximate time of the attempt to support.

Profile access

The advisor is available only after account sign-in and explicit consent to the collection and processing of data described in the Privacy Policy. Profile information is used only under the permissions granted by the user.

Revoke access

Use ChatGPT's app or connection settings to disconnect the advisor. Revoking host access stops future calls from that connection; it does not by itself delete records already held by DTI.

Access, correction or deletion

Send a request from the email associated with your connected account to contact@darivia.com. Additional verification may be required before account-related information is disclosed or deleted.

Current release boundaries

Some connected functions in the initial public release remain demonstrations unless a specific live integration is disclosed.

The advisor does not complete purchases, process payments or replace specialist advice in regulated fields.
