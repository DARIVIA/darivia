Support

Help with access to your DARIVIA Consumer Profile, privacy requests and unexpected advisor behaviour.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Contact

Email contact@darivia.com. Include a concise description, approximate time, platform and device. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card data, government ID numbers, medical records, personal health facts or full conversation exports. Redact screenshots. Support correspondence is separate from the Consumer Profile.

Connection or processing problems

Check that DARIVIA is available in the chat. Start connection from the plugin and choose “Authorize and continue” on DARIVIA's disclosure page. Sign in or register with your email and password in the WorkOS-hosted DARIVIA form. No first or last name is required. Use the form's password-recovery option if needed; do not send a password or code to support. Complete the connection within ten minutes. A successful connection should return to the initiating chat or app. A processing failure is not necessarily an authentication failure; repeated reconnection will not correct every error.

What authorization allows

The notice before the grant explains profile and structured decision-history creation, use, saving and updates for this and future authorized choices, necessary data categories and service providers. Without authorization, the connected advisor cannot start. Optional physical details may be declined; restricted data are not permitted even with consent. See the Privacy Policy.

DARIVIA Consumer Profile review and correction

Ask the advisor in ChatGPT to show your current DARIVIA Consumer Profile. Correct relevant information through the ordinary conversation; the connected service will process it through the same update mechanism used during expert guidance.

Withdraw access

Ask DARIVIA to sign you out to withdraw its grants for this account across supported clients. Disconnecting one host connection is narrower and does not erase your DARIVIA records. Revoking a WorkOS-side authorization can take up to five minutes to affect an already issued DARIVIA access credential; refresh checks WorkOS again. Reconnecting requires authorization and returns to the same WorkOS-owned DARIVIA identity.

Deletion and privacy requests

Send a request from the email associated with your connected account to contact@darivia.com. Additional verification may be required before account-related information is disclosed or deleted.

Current release boundaries

The public release is being prepared. Medical-purpose choices, medical goods, clinical services and health-insurance workflows, comparisons and recommendations are outside DARIVIA's scope. Other excluded purposes are listed in the Terms of Service. A scope refusal does not erase your records or prevent access-control and privacy requests.

The advisor does not complete purchases, process payments or provide unsupported regulated advice. An ordinary clothing or frame-only choice can use necessary nonmedical fit information; the same tool must not be used to select treatment or prescription lenses.

History and feedback

A new chat or disconnected grant does not erase your retained profile, trajectories or version history. Deletion requests remain a separate privacy-control process; no automatic expiry is introduced. The optional feedback form is offered no more than once in 365 days per user across chats and applications. It has three Yes/No answers and an optional comment, stores feedback separately, and does not replace an earlier annual review.
